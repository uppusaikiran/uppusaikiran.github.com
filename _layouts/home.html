About Me

I am a security engineer and technical leader at Adobe specializing in AI security, agentic systems, and large-scale security platforms. Over the past seven years, I have designed and built production systems that turn complex security telemetry into prioritized investigations, automated controls, and faster response.

At Adobe, I architected Project Enola, a patent-pending agentic security platform that correlated 3.94 billion SIEM events and delivered more than 5,800 investigations across 18 teams. My work also includes multi-agent security automation, enterprise monitoring for LLM and RAG systems, vulnerability-prioritization pipelines, and data-protection controls operating across high-volume telemetry. I set technical direction, mentor senior team members, and work across security, engineering, and leadership teams to turn emerging risks into deployable controls.

My focus is the intersection of AI security, distributed security platforms, application security, and threat intelligence. I work on securing LLM and agentic systems against prompt injection, jailbreaks, data leakage, poisoned context, and unsafe automation. I translate those defenses into practical guardrails for enterprise engineering teams.

I also share this work through technical writing and speaking, including the Databricks Data + AI Summit, IEEE Silicon Valley Cybersecurity Conference, Application Security Weekly, The Diana Initiative, and university security programs.
