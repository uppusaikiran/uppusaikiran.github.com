About Me

I am a Senior Security Researcher in Adobe's Cybersecurity Threat Research & Intelligence team, where I focus on AI-driven threat intelligence and cloud security. I architect and run Adobe's threat intelligence and fleet sweep platform and have built agentic AI threat intel pipelines to stay ahead of adversaries. I am the author of the Adobe Security Blog article Building an agentic AI-powered threat intelligence platform (March 2026). I am a 2026 Gold Award winner for AI Security Innovator of the Year at the Cybersecurity Excellence Awards and have placed first in the Recorded Future CTF at RSA Conference. I hold an MS in Security Informatics from Johns Hopkins and am certified in CISSP, GCTI, and AWS Security - Specialty. I have spoken at IEEE SVCC, The Diana Initiative, BSides SLC, Penn State, and UMBC Cyber Defense Lab, and was invited by Johns Hopkins as a panelist for Security Metamorphosis 2026. I have judged for the Monterey Cyber 9/12 Strategy Challenge and student hackathons.

I also hold a B.Tech in Computer Science from Vellore Institute of Technology (India). I am passionate about designing large-scale secure systems with a focus on Cloud Security, Application Security, and Threat Intelligence. My expertise includes detecting cloud threats at scale, developing automated security pipelines, and implementing proactive threat hunting frameworks. I have worked extensively with AWS environments, aligning cloud infrastructure with enterprise security policies and strengthening security controls.

My recent focus has been on Large Language Models (LLMs), particularly LLM security. I am interested in understanding and mitigating security vulnerabilities in these systems, including attacks like jailbreaking and prompt injection. I work on defenses against adversarial techniques that attempt to bypass safety guardrails in LLMs, and on the broader implications of these technologies for cybersecurity. I actively contribute to open-source projects and regularly participate in Capture the Flag competitions and hackathons with the handle backdoorcipher.
